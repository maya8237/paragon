Accessibility statement


:general
We invest a lot of resources to make this site accessible, recognizing the importance of the site's accessibility to the general population and in particular to
those with disabilities
 
:Enabling accessibility
A - Accessibility can be activated by clicking Caps Lock. This click will allow you to browse the website in an accessible and easy way and navigate between the website pages
B - Increase display by 25% by pressing the Ctrl key and the + key
C- Increase display by 50% by pressing the Ctrl key and the + key and so on
 D- Reduce the display by 25% by pressing the Ctrl key and the - key
E- Increase display by 50% by pressing the Ctrl key and the E key and so on
F- Pressing Esc will close the accessibility navigation
G- Pressing SPACE will scroll the site down
H- Pressing Ctrl + 7 will disable the flashing on the screen
I - Pressing Ctrl + 6 will activate high contrast change
J- Pressing Ctrl + 5 will activate a black and yellow display
K- Pressing Ctrl + 4 will reset all settings
 
:clarification
We make great efforts to include all surfers and those with disabilities, but you may find elements that are not accessible well enough, so if you encounter difficulty surfing or viewing the site's content, we apologize in advance, and ask that you draw your attention to it, and we will take care of it as soon as possible
